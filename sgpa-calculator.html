  • English — Credit 3 — Grade Point 8

SGPA Formula

Credit Point = Credit × Grade Point
Total Credit Points = Sum of all Credit Points
Total Credits = Sum of all Subject Credits
SGPA = Total Credit Points ÷ Total Credits
